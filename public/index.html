Prices are for raw, near-mint cards: TCGplayer’s market price (recent sales). Prices marked “live” come straight from TCGplayer through the local server; the rest come from the free TCGdex API, refreshed daily. For played cards, the listings link opens TCGplayer filtered to that condition, printing and English, so you can see what they’re actually selling for. Change a card’s condition or printing in the table and its link updates. Put what you found into “Your price” and it replaces the near-mint price in the totals. Your list, lookups and prices are saved in this browser, so they’re still here after a refresh.
